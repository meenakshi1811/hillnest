# Blog cover photos

The article covers in `public/images/blog/` are free photos from Unsplash, used under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial use, no permission or
credit required. Each photo page was checked to say "Free to use under the Unsplash License".
They were cropped to 1200×750 and saved as JPG.

None of them shows HillNest's own village, cows or ghee, so the alt text describes only what is
in the picture. Only one free photo on Unsplash or Pexels could be confirmed as ghee from its own
page (the jar below), so it is used, cropped two ways, for the two articles that need ghee on the
cover. Photos tagged "ghee" that turned out to be oil, honey, custard or orange juice were left out.

Replace any of them with your own photo from Admin → Blog → edit article → Cover photo.
