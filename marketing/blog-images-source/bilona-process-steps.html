The bilona way

From milk to ghee in 5 steps

How bilona ghee is made in village homes of Upper Shimla.

1

Fresh milk

Collected each morning from hill-grazing cows, then boiled

2

Curd overnight

Set overnight, traditionally in earthen pots

3

Hand churning

A wooden bilona separates butter from buttermilk

4

Slow clarifying

Butter heated gently over a wood fire

5

Into the jar

Strained, cooled and sealed by hand

About a day and a half from milk to jarhillnest.co
