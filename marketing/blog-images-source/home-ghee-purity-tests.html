At a glance

5 home tests for pure ghee

Use them as warning signs, not as proof.

Heat test

Melts fast, turns clear golden, smells nutty. Pale, slow or oily is a warning sign.

Iodine test

2–3 drops of tincture of iodine on melted ghee. Blue means starch. Throw it away after.

Fridge test

Melt in a glass jar and chill. Separate layers can point to another fat.

Smell & taste

Fresh and nutty is good. Sour, stale or "chemical" is not.

Label check

FSSAI licence, full address, batch number, best-before date, simple ingredients.

Still unsure?

An NABL-accredited food lab can test for vanaspati and oil blends.
